    The result is only as strong as its validation and its data gaps. Both are listed in full. No Artemis polygon or Barker tile is invented when authoritative geometry is absent.

    Provenance

    Terrain: NASA Moon Trek LRO LOLA south-polar DEM, server-resampled to 148.44 m/px. Geometry: JPL Horizons body-fixed sub-solar and sub-Earth points plus Moon-centre range, sampled every six hours from 2024-01-01 through 2026-01-01. PSR edge distance: equivalent-circle approximation from the supplied centroid-and-area catalogue.
