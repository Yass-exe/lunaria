07 · METHOD & CAVEATS

Transparent enough to argue with.

Eight criteria, explicit weights, published provenance and an uncertainty model. The output is a screening recommendation, not a flight-certified site.

The score

S site = Σ wi · si

Each criterion is normalised to 0–100 with a documented scale, then multiplied by its profile weight. Weights sum to 1. The result is a relative decision score, not a probability of mission success.

Evidence tiers

  • A
    Ground truth. Apollo 17 surface data and traverse validation.
  • B
    Measured / documented. DEM terrain, Horizons ephemerides, PSR catalogue.
  • C
    Analyst judgment. Science value, thermal, safety and data-maturity rubrics.

Tier C scores are the weakest link. The sensitivity chart samples them at ±10 points to show how much the ranking could move.

Lighting, not lightning

The brief said “lightning.” The Moon has no appreciable atmosphere, so there is no weather lightning. This analysis interprets the term as solar lighting / illumination: the fraction of the year the Sun clears the local terrain horizon. Electrostatic dust lofting is a separate, real phenomenon and is not modelled.
